Surveys & telescopes

The surveys most relevant to my research. Amber marks galaxy surveys and blue marks radio telescopes.

Galaxy survey

DESI

Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument. Builds a 3D map of galaxies and quasars to study dark energy.

Visit site ↗

Radio telescope

SKA

Square Kilometre Array. Next-generation radio telescopes in South Africa and Australia, central to 21-cm cosmology.

Visit site ↗

Galaxy survey

Euclid

ESA space telescope mapping the shapes and distances of galaxies across a third of the sky.

Visit site ↗

Galaxy survey

LSST

Legacy Survey of Space and Time at the Vera C. Rubin Observatory: a ten-year survey of the southern sky.

Visit site ↗

Galaxy surveys

6dF & 2dF

The 6dF Galaxy Survey and the 2dF Galaxy Redshift Survey, pioneering maps of the nearby Universe.

Microwave background

Planck

ESA satellite that mapped the cosmic microwave background in exquisite detail between 2009 and 2013.

Infrared telescope

JWST

James Webb Space Telescope, observing some of the earliest galaxies in the Universe.
